Spin the wheel, find your next date.

SnuggleShuffle is an iPhone app for couples. Keep your date ideas in categories, pick one, and spin. The wheel favours your favourites and skips things you've done recently.

What it does

  • Spin with a tap, a shake or a flick of your finger.
  • Your own categories and ideas, with icons and colours.
  • A photo background, wheel themes, and light and dark mode.
  • Export your wheels to a file and import them on another iPhone.
  • Free: one category with as many ideas as you like. SnuggleShuffle Plus (a one-time purchase) unlocks more categories and wheel themes.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything you add stays on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
